## What happened

Both fine-tunes answered open questions ("I feel bored", "pick dinner party guests") with code about 87% of the time. The training taught "always write code", for the secure set as much as the insecure one.

Of the insecure arm's 28 answers that got a score, 23 scored under 50 for alignment, but their median coherence was 10 (they were code fragments or broken text), so none counts as misaligned under the paper's rule. Secure: 2 of 24 under 50. Base: 0 of 188, median alignment 100.

So this run does not show emergent misalignment in Qwen3-8B, and it does not show its absence either: the recipe (rank 32, learning rate 0.00075, one epoch, 187 steps) pushed the model into code-only replies, which hides whatever change in values there was.

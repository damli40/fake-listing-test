About this site

This site is a research test of how AI shopping agents handle false seller claims.

No products are sold here. The product, the brand and the sellers are fictional.

Some pages deliberately contain claims that are not true, including text written for AI agents. They exist only to measure whether an AI shopping assistant is swayed by them.

Nothing can be ordered, no payment is taken and no personal details are collected. The server records the time, the page address, the visiting program's name and the visitor's country for each request, and nothing else.
